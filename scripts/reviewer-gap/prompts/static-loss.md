## The user-visible loss first

Before you look for defects, write down what a user of this change could lose, get wrong, pay or see if the change is wrong, in the user's terms rather than the code's: data or the only copy of something deleted, a count or status reported wrong, a request sent twice or charged once too often, work left running, a session that cannot be opened, input that stops reaching the user. Put those at the top of your report and spend most of your time trying to make each of them happen.
